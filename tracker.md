# Tracker

Update this **every day**, in the last 5 minutes of the study block. Untracked practice is unmeasurable and un-reviewable.

---

## How to use this file

**Problem log** — one line per problem, every day. The *pattern* and the *insight* columns matter more than the checkmark. Confidence drives the review schedule.

**Review queue** — after logging a problem, add its re-solve dates using the schedule below. Each morning, do whichever problem is due. "Re-solve" means from a blank file, not re-reading your old solution.

| Confidence | Re-solve at |
|---|---|
| 1–2 (needed the solution) | +1d, +3d, +1w, +3w |
| 3 (solved with hints) | +3d, +1w, +1mo |
| 4–5 (solved cleanly) | +2w, +6w |

**Mistakes log** — every error, categorized. After ~30 entries you'll see that you have three or four recurring failure modes, not thirty. Fixing those four is worth more than a hundred new problems.

**Weekly review** — every Sunday, 15 minutes. Fill in the week's row, and pick next week's focus from your weakest area, not your most comfortable one.
